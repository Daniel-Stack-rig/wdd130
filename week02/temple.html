About the Temple

The Abidjan Ivory Coast Temple is the seventh temple built in Africa and the first for Ivory Coast (Côte d'Ivoire).

Dedicated 25 May 2025: Dedicatory Prayer

Temple District & Visitor Tips

  • Nearest accommodation: Temple patron housing (booking required).
  • Local transportation: Taxis and ride-hailing services available from central Abidjan.
  • Dining: Several restaurants near Riviera Golf area, 5–10 min drive.
  • Ordinance scheduling: Advance reservation recommended via Church account.
  • Dress code: Modest attire requested on temple grounds.

For current temple closures or weather alerts, please visit the official Temple Schedule page.
