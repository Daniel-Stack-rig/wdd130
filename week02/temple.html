About the Temple

The Abidjan Ivory Coast Temple is the seventh temple built in Africa and the first for Ivory Coast (Côte d'Ivoire).

Dedicated 25 May 2025: Dedicatory Prayer

Visiting Information

Temple Facts & History

The Abidjan Ivory Coast Temple serves over 45,000 members in Ivory Coast and neighboring countries. It features traditional Latter-day Saint temple architecture with beautiful African influences.

  • Announced: April 5, 2020
  • Groundbreaking: December 12, 2021
  • Dedicated: May 25, 2025
  • Site: 3.5 acres
  • Ordinance Rooms: 2 instruction rooms, 2 sealing rooms
